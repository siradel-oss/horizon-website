Horizon can displayed [tens of dynamically-streamed rasters](/doc/multiple_rasters) spanning the entire planet,
down to centimeter resolution, as imagery or [terrain heightmaps](/doc/dtm_raster) (DTM).

Rasters containing data values (such as simulation results) can be [dynamically
colorized by providing a palette](/doc/palettized_image_raster).

The [terrain](/doc/terrain_settings) itself can be made semi transparent to reveal underground objects.
